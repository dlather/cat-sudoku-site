Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

The short version: Cat Sudoku does not collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your game progress (which levels you have solved, your daily streak, any puzzle you left half-finished) and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No network requests: all puzzles are built into the app.

Sharing your results

If you tap Share result, your device's standard share sheet opens with a short text summary of your time and score. Where that text goes is entirely up to you. We never see it.

Ratings

The app may occasionally ask whether you'd like to rate it. That prompt is shown and handled by Apple; we receive no information from it beyond what Apple publishes on the App Store.

Children

Cat Sudoku is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding optional analytics, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
