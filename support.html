How to play

Each board is split into coloured regions. Find the hidden cats:

  • one cat in every row and every column,
  • one cat in every colour,
  • no two cats touching, not even diagonally.

Tap a square once to cross it out, and again to place a cat. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, with no guessing needed.

Common questions

The later levels are really hard. Is that normal?
Yes. Every level is harder than the one before, and from level 30 the game stays at its hardest. Each new chapter needs a new technique; if you're stuck, the Hint button reveals the next cat you could have worked out.
Can the game cross out squares for me?
Yes. Turn on Auto-mark squares in Settings, and squares ruled out by a cat you've placed are crossed out automatically.
How do I reset my progress?
Open Settings in the game and tap Reset progress. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app also resets it.
Does the daily puzzle change for everyone at the same time?
Everyone gets the same puzzle for the same date, and it changes at midnight in your own time zone.
